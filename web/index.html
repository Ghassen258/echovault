HOW TO BREAK TIME

Reach the green extraction portal by opening gates with pressure plates. To get off a plate without closing its gate, record yourself standing on it and let your echo take over.

01WALK TO A PLATE

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the touch pad.

02REWIND TIME

Press R to create an echo recording and return to the start.

03COOPERATE WITH YOURSELF

Your echo repeats its journey, then stays on its last tile. Wait with Space or the Wait button to synchronize.

04OPEN THE VAULT

Pass the open gate to the extraction point. Use C to reset a level; N to continue after clearing it.
